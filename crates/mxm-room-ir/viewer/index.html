Rooms Behind the Impulses

Every impulse response in the catalogue comes from a room built as code: watertight polygons, one published absorption blend per surface, a source and two receivers. These are fifteen of the hundred, exactly as the solver sees them — pews, furniture, bars and boulders included. Drag to turn a room over; scroll to move closer.

Surfaces

    Geometry is generic — no archetype copies a real building. A surface that stands for geometry left out of the model (coffers, piers, window recesses) carries that depth as scattering instead; where the shape matters acoustically it is built as a solid, which is why the stage, the galleries, the halls' pilasters and ribs, a church's pew rows, a pub's bar and a cave's boulders are here as real faces.

    Written by catalogue -- geometry <slug>, which exports any of the hundred rooms as a Wavefront OBJ into target\mxm-room-ir\geometry\.
